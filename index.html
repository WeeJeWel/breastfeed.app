BreastFeed

One less thing to remember.

Remember the last feed and which side is next. Log breastfeeding, bottles and pumping in one simple journal, shared with your partner.

Download on the App Store
iCloud Sharing

Share the night shift.

Invite your partner to your baby’s journal through iCloud. You can both add and edit feeds, so whoever takes over knows when the last feed happened.

One shared history. No separate account to create.

Live Activities

A glance is all it takes.

Keep the feeding timer and current side on your Lock Screen. Finish a feed or switch sides right from the Live Activity, without reopening the app.

Less tapping when your hands are full.

Apple Watch

Right there on your wrist.

Start a left or right feed, log a bottle, or record pumping from Apple Watch. Pick your baby and keep the journal up to date without reaching for your iPhone.

Big buttons. A few quick taps.

Everything is free.

Every feature is included. No subscriptions, in-app purchases or ads. Babies are expensive enough as it is.
